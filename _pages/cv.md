Research Interest
======
My research interests lie in two closely related directions. The first is **quantum algorithms**, particularly for problems motivated by *optimization*, such as graph and discrete optimization problems [4,5,6], as well as distributional and learning-related tasks [2,8,10,11]; I am also interested in *quantum algorithms for quantum dynamics and simulation* [1,9]. The second is **quantum program logic**, especially *relational* quantum Hoare logics [3,7], where I am interested in bringing in tools from *quantum optimal transport* and other optimization-based methods to develop new logical and semantic results.

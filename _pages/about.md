I am a Ph.D. student at the Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences, advised by Prof. Mingsheng Ying and Prof. Zhengfeng Ji. I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Technology from Yao Class, Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences, Tsinghua University in 2021.

Research
======

My research interests lie in quantum algorithms and quantum program logic.

In quantum algorithms, I am particularly interested in problems motivated by optimization, including graph and discrete optimization problems, distributional and learning-related tasks, and quantum algorithms for quantum dynamics and simulation.

In quantum program logic, I am interested in relational quantum Hoare logics, quantum optimal transport, and optimization-based methods for developing logical and semantic results for quantum programs.
